# Animated-Brand-Logo
I created an animated version of a local Denver outdoor gear store’s logo to help them go national.<br><br>In the year 2026, it’s absolutely essential for every brand to utilize motion across their digital branding, and as the most visible part of their branding, the logo is of course no exception. 

To complete this animated logo for FERAL, a gear shop in Denver who I’m working with to scale heir used gear sales, and their brand, nationally, I began by pulling a vector of their logo into Adobe Illustrator. I then split its logo up into its parts: the individual letters and the two ice climbing axes. I the. moved the logo into Principle via Figma and got to work animating its pieces. 

I knew I wanted the logo to “climb” the screen, as if the word FERAL was using the ice axes to climb into position. I animated the swinging axes, attempting to emulate the motion of an ice climber. I ran into some issues that held me up for a while until I could resolve them, but after scrapping and restarting the axe animations a few times, I finally got a version that felt right. Then, I added the slide in of the word FERAL from the bottom of the screen and a simple fade-in of “Denver, CO.” While it could still use some refinement if it were to be used by the client, it’d make a fun bonus deliverable.

The process of creating this animation reminded me just how easily projects with multiple nested components can go south if you aren’t deliberate. I plan on animating every logo I create going forward that might find itself on a screen, and I hope to refine my ability with repetition.
